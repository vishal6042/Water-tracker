# Privacy Policy — Water Tracker

_Last updated: 2026-07-29_

Water Tracker is designed to respect your privacy.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Water Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no network
requests for your data.

## Where your data lives

All of your data — your daily glass goal, logged glasses and their times, your history,
and your reminder settings — is stored **only on your device** using local storage. It
never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

## Notifications

If you enable **Reminders**, the app schedules local notifications on your device to
remind you to drink water. These are generated entirely on-device; no reminder data is
sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app, and you control the
notification permission in your device settings.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — used solely to show the water reminders you opt into.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
